Try it in 60 seconds

  1. /try: one real order in about 5 seconds on the bot's own testnet sandbox account. 12 USDso, closed by itself within 10 minutes; you get the tx link.
  2. /demo: the bot's own live account, its last orders with tx links and the live Reactivity streams. No wallet needed.
  3. /stops 0xAnyAccount: the dreamDEX TP/SL the chain holds for that account, and whether each one will close what it should. Read only.
  4. /watch 0xAnyAccount: liquidation pings for any account. Read only.
  5. /start, Link a bot in the dreamDEX app, then /long ETH 20 5x tp 4% sl 2%.

Chain-fired stop-loss: closed by Somnia Reactivity while the bot was stopped

The demo account 0x2A11…bF08 points its code at ExitGuard (our 9967-byte contract, source) with one EIP-7702 transaction, and arms a stop on itself. From then on Somnia Reactivity wakes the account every 5 s (a one-shot Schedule it renews on every wake) and every epoch; when the mark crosses the level, the account closes its own position. Nobody signs that close: it is a reactive transaction the validators add to the block. This run was made with the Telegram bot stopped.

This is ExitGuard version 3. Version 1 woke on every new order on the book and would have cost up to ~36 STT an hour on a busy book; version 2 never renewed its schedule. Both are retired; their transactions are in evidence/exitguard_ops.jsonl and what each taught us is in the README.

Limits: one stop per account (one market) at a time; a check every 5 s, each paid by the account (about 0.0026 STT, ~1.8 STT an hour), and the account must keep 32 STT to renew its wake-up (below that the epoch heartbeat still checks every ~5 minutes); the level here was set 0.06 % under the mark so it would fire within minutes for this run. Testnet only.

Self-healing chain link

On 2026-10-27 the bot's WebSocket client stopped reconnecting and every chain read failed for about 28 hours while the process looked healthy. Since 2026-10-29 a watchdog probes the chain every 30 s: 6 failed checks in a row rebuild the client and re-subscribe the Reactivity streams, 6 more stop the bot so systemd restarts it, and /status shows when the link was last verified. A test reproduces the failure with the real SDK and a local WebSocket server and shows the rebuild heals it.
